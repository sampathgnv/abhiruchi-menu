Privacy Policy — Abhiruchi Sweets app

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This policy explains how the Abhiruchi Sweets Android application ("the app") handles information. The app is published by Sree Bhaktanjaneya Abhiruchi Sweets, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, India.

In short: the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you.

We do not collect personal data

The app has no accounts, no sign-up, and no login. We do not ask for, collect, or receive:

  • your name, phone number, email address, or postal address
  • your location — the app never requests location permission
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone
  • any advertising identifier or device identifier

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking or crash-reporting services, and no third-party software development kits that collect data about you.

Information stored on your own device

Your shopping cart is kept on your own phone so that it survives switching between apps. It is stored only on your device, is never sent to us, and is cleared when you close and reopen the app. You can also clear it yourself at any time using the "Clear cart" button.

We cannot see your cart.

Network use

The app is designed to work completely offline. Prices and store details are built into the app itself.

The app makes one type of network request: it periodically downloads a public price list file from our website host so that prices stay current without you needing to update the app. This request only downloads information — it sends us no information about you or your device beyond what any ordinary web request necessarily reveals to the hosting provider (such as your IP address, which we do not log, store, or have access to). If the download fails or you are offline, the app simply uses the prices already built into it.

Placing an order through WhatsApp

When you tap "Place Order via WhatsApp", the app prepares a message containing the items in your cart and opens WhatsApp with that message ready to send. The app does not send the message — you review it and choose whether to send it.

If you choose to send it, your order and your WhatsApp phone number reach us through WhatsApp, in the same way as any message you send us. We use those details only to prepare, confirm, and deliver your order, and to contact you about it. Your use of WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Any optional details you type into the app before ordering — such as your name, a note for the shop, or which of our branches is nearest to you — are placed into that WhatsApp message only. They are not stored by the app and are not sent anywhere else.

Links to other services

The app contains links that open other applications or websites, including Google Maps for directions, your phone's dialler, your email application, Swiggy, Zomato, Facebook, and our website. Once you follow such a link, the privacy policy of that service applies. We do not share any information with those services.

Children

The app is a shop catalogue suitable for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Payments

The app does not process payments and does not handle card, bank, or UPI details.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and publish the revised version at this address.
